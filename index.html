What Sayxrosnimos Does

Sayxrosnimos is a publishing and account-management tool for creators. You connect your own TikTok account, pick a video file you already created on your computer, add a caption and hashtags, and publish — without juggling browser tabs and re-uploading by hand.

👥

Multiple Accounts in One Place

Connect and switch between several of your own TikTok creator accounts from a single dashboard, so you don't have to log in and out all day.

⬆️

Upload Your Own Videos

Select video files you've already made on your PC and publish them to the connected account using the official TikTok Content Posting API.

📝

Captions & Hashtags

Write the title, description and hashtags once in the dashboard and they travel with the video when it's posted — consistent and organized.

💬

Comments & Messages

Review and reply to comments on your posts from one screen, making it easier to keep up with your audience across accounts.

🗂️

Organized Library

Every video you publish is tracked with its status, caption and metrics, so your whole catalog stays in one tidy, searchable place.

📊

Performance at a Glance

See views, likes and engagement for your published posts in a simple panel, so you know what's working without opening the TikTok app.

How We Use the TikTok API

Sayxrosnimos integrates with the official TikTok for Developers API so you can publish to your own connected account. We only request the minimum permissions needed to do that:
