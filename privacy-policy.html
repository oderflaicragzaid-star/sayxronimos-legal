Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sayxrosnimos ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Sayxrosnimos TikTok publishing and account-management application ("App").

1. What the App Is

Sayxrosnimos is a desktop tool that helps a creator manage their own TikTok account(s): connect an account, upload video files the creator already has on their computer, add captions and hashtags, publish, and review comments — all from one dashboard.

2. Information We Access

The App integrates with TikTok via the official TikTok for Developers API. In doing so, it may access:

  • User basic info (user.info.basic): your TikTok display name and open_id, used only to confirm which connected account you are publishing to.
  • Video content (video.upload, video.publish): the App uploads and posts the video files you choose, on behalf of your authenticated account.

We do not collect, store, or share personal information about any TikTok users other than the connected account owner who authorized the App.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed via the TikTok API is used exclusively to:

  • Confirm and display which connected TikTok account you are working with.
  • Upload and publish the video content you select, with the caption and hashtags you provide.
  • Show you the status and basic performance of posts you published through the App.

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Data Storage

OAuth access tokens are stored locally on the creator's own device in a configuration file. The App does not run a cloud service and does not store your TikTok data on external servers.

5. TikTok API Compliance

The Sayxrosnimos App complies with TikTok's Platform Privacy and Security Guidelines. We request only the minimum permissions necessary: user.info.basic, video.upload, and video.publish.

6. Your Rights

You may revoke the Sayxrosnimos App's access to your TikTok account at any time by visiting TikTok Settings → Security → Authorized apps and removing the app.
