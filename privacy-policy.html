Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sayxrosnimos ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Sayxrosnimos TikTok content automation application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

The Sayxrosnimos App integrates with TikTok via the official TikTok for Developers API. In doing so, we may access:

  • User basic info: TikTok display name and open_id, used solely to verify account identity before posting content.
  • Video data: We upload pre-generated video files to TikTok on behalf of the authenticated account.

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from TikTok users other than the authenticated creator account owner.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed via TikTok API is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate and verify the connected TikTok creator account.
  • Publish educational video content about stoicism and psychology to the connected account.

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

OAuth access tokens are stored locally on the creator's own device in an encrypted configuration file. No user data is stored on external servers.

4. TikTok API Compliance

The Sayxrosnimos App complies with TikTok's Platform Privacy and Security Guidelines. We only request the minimum permissions necessary: video.publish, video.upload, and user.info.basic.

5. Your Rights

You may revoke the Sayxrosnimos App's access to your TikTok account at any time by visiting TikTok Settings → Security → Authorized apps and removing the app.

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at: sayxronimos@gmail.com
